About AquaDose

AquaDose makes Estimative Index (EI) dry salt dosing effortless for planted freshwater aquariums. Enter your tank volume in Liters (L) or US Gallons (gal), choose a preset (Low, Medium, or High) or customize your target Nitrate (NO3), Phosphate (PO4), and Potassium (K) ppm levels, and get instant gram measurements.

Key Features

  • Dry Salt Calculations: Exact gram weights for Potassium Nitrate (KNO3), Monopotassium Phosphate (KH2PO4), and Potassium Sulfate (K2SO4).
  • Smart Potassium Balancing: Automatically accounts for potassium contributed by KNO3 and KH2PO4 before calculating remaining K2SO4.
  • Metric & Imperial Units: Switch seamlessly between Liters (L) and US Gallons (gal).
  • 100% Offline Calculation Engine: All dosing calculations run locally on your device.
