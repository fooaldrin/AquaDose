Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

AquaDose ("the App") is developed and maintained by Thomas Aldrin. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Local Calculation Data

The App does not require user accounts, logins, or registration. All aquarium volume inputs and nutrient target settings are processed locally on your device.

2. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat & Apple StoreKit)

AquaDose offers an optional one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase ("Remove Ads"). Purchases and restores are processed securely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit) and validated via RevenueCat using anonymous app user identifiers to verify your entitlement status. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. Advertising

For users on the free version of the App, advertisements may be displayed via Google AdMob. If you purchase the "Remove Ads" upgrade, all banner advertisements are permanently disabled. When ads are active, AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers, ad interaction metrics, and crash diagnostics in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms and your iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) settings.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not operate custom external databases storing personal user profiles. Uninstalling the App removes all local preferences from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Thomas Aldrin at thomas@thomasaldrin.com or visit our Support Page.
