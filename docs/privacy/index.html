Privacy Policy

App: AquaDose

Last updated: 2026-10-05

Overview

AquaDose is a hydration tracking and wellness app. The app does not require account creation and does not ask users to directly provide personal profile information.

Data the app may process

  • Hydration logs and personal health settings stored locally on your device for app functionality.
  • Optional health data (age, weight, activity level) entered by the user for hydration recommendations, stored locally only.
  • Advertising-related data may be processed by the ad provider (Google Mobile Ads), such as device identifiers, diagnostics, and approximate location data, according to Google policies.
  • Basic technical diagnostics (for ad delivery and stability) through third-party SDKs.

Advertising

AquaDose uses Google Mobile Ads to show ads. Ad delivery and related data use are governed by Google's privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

How data is used

  • To run core hydration tracking features.
  • To save and restore local hydration logs and user preferences.
  • To provide personalized hydration reminders and recommendations.
  • To display ads and maintain ad service quality.

Data sharing

The app does not sell personal data. Limited data may be processed by third-party ad providers as needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention. Hydration logs and personal health data are never shared with external services.

Data storage and security

All hydration logs and personal health information are stored locally on your device. Data is not transmitted to external servers or cloud services. If you uninstall the app, your data will be removed from your device.

Children's privacy

AquaDose is not directed specifically to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data inappropriately, contact support to request review.

Your choices

  • You can stop app data processing by uninstalling the app.
  • You can clear your hydration logs within the app settings at any time.
  • You can manage ad/privacy controls via your device settings where available.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/fooaldrin/AquaDose/issues.

This page is provided for App Store compliance and user transparency. Update it whenever data practices or SDK usage changes.
