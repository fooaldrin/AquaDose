About & Support Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

AquaDose ("the App") is developed and maintained by Thomas Aldrin. This page informs users regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data for anyone using the service.

1. Data Collection & Usage

The App does not require user accounts, logins, or contact registration. All tank configurations, nutrient targets, and dosing schedules are stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development, AquaDose displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous information to serve relevant ad content and maintain system telemetry, including:

  • Identifiers: Device identifiers (such as IDFV or Advertising Identifier).
  • Usage Data: Ad views, clicks, and user interactions.
  • Diagnostics: Performance metrics and SDK-related crash logs.

This information is not linked to your personal identity. For more information on how Google processes ad data, refer to Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS devices, AquaDose respects system-level tracking preferences. If you opt out of tracking via the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, Google AdMob will serve non-personalized, contextual advertisements without utilizing your advertising identifier (IDFA) across third-party apps.

4. Security & Data Retention

Because your aquarium data never leaves your device, we retain no personal data on external servers. Clearing your application data or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local calculation history.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact: privacy@thomasaldrin.com.
