# AquaDose

**Estimative Index (EI) aquarium dosing calculator for iOS.**

AquaDose is an offline mobile utility designed specifically for planted freshwater aquarium hobbyists. It eliminates guesswork and complex spreadsheets by calculating precise dry salt fertilizer dosages using the **Estimative Index (EI)** methodology.

## Key Features

* **Instant Dry Salt Calculations:** Get exact gram measurements for Potassium Nitrate (`KNO3`), Monopotassium Phosphate (`KH2PO4`), and Potassium Sulfate (`K2SO4`).
* **Smart Potassium Balancing:** Automatically accounts for the potassium (`K`) contributed by `KNO3` and `KH2PO4` before calculating the remaining `K2SO4` needed to reach your target ppm.
* **One-Tap EI Presets & Custom PPM Targets:** Switch between Low, Medium, and High Estimative Index profiles or customize Nitrate (`NO3`), Phosphate (`PO4`), and Potassium (`K`) ppm targets.
* **Metric & Imperial Support:** Calculate dosages in Liters (`L`) or US Gallons (`gal`).
* **100% Offline Core:** All dosage calculations execute locally on your device with no mandatory account creation or cloud dependencies.
* **Optional Remove Ads Upgrade:** Permanently remove banner advertisements with a one-time In-App Purchase (`$9.99 USD`).
